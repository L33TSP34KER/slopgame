## Verdict (mimo)

Coverage: **16 rigs x 4 states x 4 facings x 6 frames = 1,536 renders, 0 render
errors, no missing facing anywhere.** Walk/attack/death animate for 15 of 16 rigs.

Confirmed defects:
1. **ballista attack is frozen in every direction** (1 unique frame of 6, d0/d2/d4/d6).
   Root cause found: `unit_art.py` gives the ballista `kind="machine"` — a
   procedural rig with **no attack pose at all**, so firing looks like standing
   still. Fix: synthesise a recoil (arm rotation ±10°, chassis kick 3–4 px,
   2-frame bolt/flash) — a siege engine needs the kick more than a walk cycle.
2. `ballista idle` is also 1/6 (static). Acceptable for a machine if attack
   moves; otherwise add the same recoil skeleton at low amplitude.

Retracted (my heuristic, verified false positives):
- `idle: 4 unique frames` on humanoid rigs is a breathing idle — correct
  behaviour, not a bug.
- `fiend/attack` 22 px and `hollow-king/attack` 16 px "feet swing" is pose lean
  measured on the image bbox, not the draw anchor; both animate with 4 unique
  frames (`anim-fiend-perdir.png`). With per-frame ox/oy anchoring (per
  flare-import.md) this should not slide in-game — **verify visually in play**,
  do not "fix" it blind.

`anim-ballista-perdir.png`, `anim-fiend-perdir.png`.
