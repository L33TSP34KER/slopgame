## Open findings (priority order)

1. **NO END-STATE / stalemate risk.** In the 10-minute autoplay session the
   player army fell to 3 units while the enemy host grew to **106** and the
   game stayed `state=play` the whole time — no defeat (hall alive), no
   victory (no push). A human who loses their army would sit in the same
   limbo. Needs: defeat when production + hall are gone, and an AI that
   finishes the game (or an objective/wave cap that forces resolution).
2. **Enemy population is unbounded** — 29 → 45 → 63 → 76 → 90 → 106 across
   16 in-game minutes with no cap. Even with fps fine today, each wave spawn
   is a growing hitch and the minimap turns into confetti.
3. **Wave-spawn A* hitch.** `can_step` jumps from ~120/frame to **1,626 →
   2,646 → 20,706 → 22,208 per frame** for one 60-frame block at each spawn
   moment, dropping that block to 26–29 fps. One visible half-second stutter
   per wave. Fix: stagger the initial paths of newly spawned units across
   ticks (budget N path requests per tick).
4. **Siege finish is slow and trickles.** `--scene siege --autoplay`: the
   Necropolis holds 5200 HP until t≈135 and then falls ~19 HP/s of game time
   → 3175 HP left at t=243; meanwhile the player's own army drains 27 → 14
   as units arrive one at a time and die. Attack in waves, not singles, or
   the siege never converts to the victory screen.
5. **Buildings static in `play`** (9 → 8 → 9 over 6 in-game minutes): the
   autoplay controller is training units but not expanding its base; confirm
   the farm→barracks→footman chain also *builds* (it does per deepseek's own
   verification, but my run shows no growth after the first minute).
